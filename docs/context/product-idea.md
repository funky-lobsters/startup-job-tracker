# Our Project Idea: Startup/VC Job Tracker

Separate from `course-requirements.md` (course requirements) and `course-sprints.md` (sprint checklists). This file is the product idea and gets refined over time.

**Tagline:** A two-sided startup jobs platform that tracks opportunities, lists candidates and matches the right talent with the right early-stage teams.

## Major user journeys

### Job seeker (Phase 1)

I sign up and fill in my profile with my background, skills and the kind of startup roles I'm interested in. I log in and see a feed of recommended jobs and companies matched to my profile. I open a job post, see the company's details, the requirements and any contacts we have in common. I save the role to my tracker, apply automatically through the link, and status updates automatically as I move through the process.

### Founder or startup hiring manager (Phase 2)

I log in and claim my company's profile in the company roster. I post a new role with the description, requirements and contact details. I browse the candidate repository, filter by background and interests, and see AI-suggested candidates who fit the role. I shortlist the people I like and reach out directly.

## Core features

The site is split into a landing page with three core sections:

1. Companies
2. Jobs
3. People

## Instructor feedback (Kyle, on our proposal)

> You might focus instead on just aggregating start-up jobs. There's near-zero probability that start-ups will use this (there are so many, they're hard to reach, and they are disorganized). You can use Yale's Crunchbase and other subs to get lists of start-ups, crawl their websites, etc. An internal tool could be great. Happy to help with this.

**What this means for us (current direction):**

- Focus on the **job seeker side**: aggregating startup jobs for MBA students (like us) looking for startup/VC roles.
- Treat the founder/hiring-manager journey (Phase 2) as **deprioritized**: don't build it unless the team explicitly decides to.
- Get startup lists from Yale's Crunchbase access and other subscriptions, and import/crawl startups' websites for job postings.
- Think of it as an **internal tool** for Yale students rather than a public two-sided marketplace.
- Kyle offered to help with this; ask him about data sources (Crunchbase access, crawling).
